ESL Crossword Race — How It Works

Step 1 — Create

Upload a CSV file with word/clue pairs, name the topic, and generate rounds. Each round splits words between two teams.

Step 2 — Freeze

Freeze rounds before class. Frozen rounds permanently match printed worksheets and cannot be edited.

Step 3 — Print

Use the Print button on each frozen worksheet. Hand out printed sheets to students.

Step 4 — Play

Select a frozen round from the dropdown. Start the tournament. Teams race to complete their crosswords!

Important: If you edit a round after printing but before freezing, the worksheet may not match the game.
